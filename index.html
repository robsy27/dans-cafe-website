Our Story

A proper local cafe, made for everyone

Dan's Cafe has been serving Whitchurch and the surrounding area since 2022 — a modern, welcoming spot for breakfast, brunch and diner-style classics, cooked fresh every day.

Whether you're in for a full English, a loaded breakfast burrito, a burger and fries, or just a coffee and a loaded milkshake, everyone's welcome at Dan's — locals, families, dog walkers and passers-through alike.

What's Cooking

The menu

Breakfast served all day, burgers off the grill, proper mains and a kids’ menu — all made with meat from our local butchers. Daily specials are always on the board in-store.

Select any menu to read it full size. GFA = gluten free available. Please note that all 14 allergens are used in our kitchen — do let a member of staff know if you have any dietary requirements.
