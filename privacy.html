Privacy & Cookies

Dan's Cafe is a small independent cafe in Whitchurch. We collect as little as possible through this website, we don't sell anything to anyone, and we don't advertise to you. This page explains exactly what happens.

Last updated 6 September 2026

Who we are

Dan's Cafe, Bridgewater Buildings, Bridgewater Street, Whitchurch SY13 1QJ. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us on 01948 258170 or danscafeinwhitchurch@gmail.com.

What this website collects

If you send us an enquiry

The contact form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally a preferred date, party size and your message. We use these only to reply to you and to arrange whatever you've asked about.

The form is delivered by a third-party service, FormSubmit, which passes your message to our email inbox. Your details pass through their system in transit. We don't use them for anything else, and your enquiry is not added to a mailing list.

Legal basis: legitimate interest — you've contacted us and we're replying.

If you agree to analytics

With your consent we use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site — which pages get looked at, roughly where visitors come from, and whether they're on a phone or a computer. It's aggregate and statistical; it doesn't tell us who you are.

Nothing is loaded until you accept. If you decline, or simply ignore the banner, no Google Analytics code is downloaded, no cookies are set and no data is sent. If you accept and later change your mind, we delete the analytics cookies straight away.

Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the button below.

Other services this page uses

Two things load from Google whether or not you accept analytics, because they are part of how the page is built. In both cases Google will see your IP address, as it would for any website using them:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces the site is set in.
  • Google Maps — the map showing where to find us. Google may set its own cookies inside that map panel, under its own privacy terms.

Neither is used by us to identify or track you, and we receive no information from either.

How long we keep things

Enquiry emails are kept in our inbox while we deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you get back in touch, then deleted. Analytics data is retained by Google under their standard retention settings for our account. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Who we share it with

Nobody, beyond the services named above that make the site work. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't share it with other businesses for marketing.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it if it's wrong, or delete it. You can object to how we're using it, and withdraw consent for analytics whenever you like. Just contact us using the details at the top — we'll respond within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we'd rather you told us first so we can put it right.
